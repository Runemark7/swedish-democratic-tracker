# Löftesstatus is vote-based, never kept or broken

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-27. Clarified 2026-10-28.

A Löfte's status states only what the party did in the Riksdag: Röstat för, Röstat emot, Blandat or Inte prövat än. The status is computed from the party's Partilinje on Bekräftade kopplingar, each of which carries a direction confirmed by a person (whether Ja or Nej is in the Löfte's direction). It is never "hållet", "brutet" or "uppfyllt". A vote can be checked against Riksdagen's own record. It is also fair to opposition parties, which cannot deliver anything on their own. And it avoids verdicts the site would have to defend, which would break the rule that fact and interpretation are kept apart. Avvikare are shown, but they never change a party's status.

A koppling where the party's Partilinje is Avstår or Delad has no direction for that party. If every Bekräftad koppling for a Löfte is like that, the Löftesstatus is Inte prövat än, and those votes are still listed on the Löfte page (decided 2026-10-28). Löftesstatus ships in Phase 2, after the Motioner work; Partilinje is built in Phase 1 and shared.

## Considered Options

- **Kept / partly / broken / not tested.** Rejected. It is a verdict about outcomes, it needs editorial judgement on every Löfte, and it is biased against parties not in government.
- **Direction inferred from who proposed the bill (the retired alignment percentage, migration 000034).** Rejected. It scored opposition parties as breaking their own promises when they voted Nej to the rejection of their own motion. Direction depends on what each förslagspunkt proposes, so a person has to confirm it per koppling.
