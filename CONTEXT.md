# Riksdagskollen

Answers one question for voters: "Vad beslutar riksdagen — och håller de vad de lovar?" Phase 1 answers the first half by following motions through to the decision. Phase 2 answers the second half with Löften. Local politics (kommun, region) is a separate side section.

## Language

**Motion**:
A proposal to the Riksdag from one or more members. It holds one or more Yrkanden and is identified by riksmöte and beteckning, e.g. 2017/18:3293.
_Avoid_: Förslag (ambiguous), Lagförslag, Bill

**Kommittémotion**:
A Motion submitted in the name of a party's members of one utskott, usually expressing the party line in that area. 2017/18:3293 (M) is one.
_Avoid_: Partimotion (that is the separate kind signed by party leadership), Committee motion (in UI copy)

**Enskild motion**:
A Motion by one or more individual members. When its signers come from more than one party, the site calls it a Flerpartimotion. Riksdagen's data has no such type, so it is derived from the signers.
_Avoid_: Privat motion, Individual motion (in UI copy)

**Yrkande**:
One numbered demand inside a Motion; the unit that the Riksdag decides on. A motion's outcome is the sum of its Yrkanden's outcomes.
_Avoid_: Krav (except in Återkommande krav), Punkt (that is a Förslagspunkt)

**Betänkande**:
An utskott's report on a matter, proposing a decision for each Förslagspunkt and giving the utskott's reasoning. It is identified by riksmöte and beteckning, e.g. 2017/18:SkU11.
_Avoid_: Ärende, Report, Committee report (in UI copy)

**Förslagspunkt**:
One numbered point in a Betänkande. The chamber decides it either by votering or by Acklamation, and it may cover Yrkanden from many Motions.
_Avoid_: Punkt alone in UI copy, Votering (a Förslagspunkt may have no votering)

**Reservation**:
A dissenting proposal in a Betänkande by the utskott members of one or more parties. It is the alternative that can be put against the utskott's proposal in a votering.
_Avoid_: Avvikande mening, Dissent; never confuse with Avvikare (a member voting against their Partilinje)

**Acklamation**:
A Förslagspunkt decided without a votering because nobody demanded one, so no per-member or per-party record exists. In UI copy: "Avgjord utan omröstning".
_Avoid_: Enhälligt (it does not mean everyone agreed), Unanimous

**Skriftlig fråga**:
A written question from a member to a minister, answered in writing, usually within a week or two. 2022/23:339 by Rickard Nordin (C), answered by Elisabeth Svantesson (M), is one.
_Avoid_: Interpellation (that is debated in the chamber), Fråga alone when the type matters

**Interpellation**:
A question from a member to a minister that is answered and debated in the chamber.
_Avoid_: Skriftlig fråga, Frågestund

**Återkommande krav**:
The same Yrkande returning across riksmöten, e.g. M's demand for an inquiry into the tax treatment of virtual goods in 2017/18:3293, 2018/19:2900 y26, 2019/20:2718 y26 and 2021/22:3638 y9. In Phase 1 only identical wording is grouped, automatically. From Phase 2, other members join only after Redaktör confirmation.
_Avoid_: Samma motion, Upprepad motion, Similar motion

**Löfte**:
A concrete, testable statement in a party's valmanifest for an election.
_Avoid_: Mål, Partimål, Manifest, Vallöfte (in UI copy)

**Valmanifest**:
A party's published election programme; the source that Löften are taken from.
_Avoid_: Manifest, Partiprogram

**Löftesstatus**:
What a party did about a Löfte, judged only from its votes: Röstat för, Röstat emot, Blandat, or Inte prövat än. If every Bekräftad koppling has Partilinje Avstår or Delad, it is Inte prövat än.
_Avoid_: Hållet, Brutet, Uppfyllt

**Bekräftad koppling**:
A link between a Löfte and a vote that the editor has confirmed; only these count toward a Löftesstatus.
_Avoid_: Match, Alignment

**Föreslagen koppling**:
An AI-suggested link between a Löfte and a vote, waiting for the editor's yes or no; never shown publicly.
_Avoid_: Unclear match

**Partilinje**:
How the majority of a party's members voted in a votering; the basis for a Löftesstatus.
_Avoid_: Party vote

**Avvikare**:
A member who voted against their Partilinje in a votering.
_Avoid_: Rebel, Utbrytare

**Mitt parti**:
The party a visitor picks to personalise the front page.
_Avoid_: Favoritparti

**Lokalt**:
The side section about kommuner and regioner, outside the core question.
_Avoid_: Kommun/Region as top-level sections

**Regeringen**:
The fifth top-menu item: who governs, the ministers, the state budget, the Riksdag overview and the agencies (myndigheter).
_Avoid_: Regering och budget, Riksdag as its own menu item

**Redaktör**:
The single person who approves Löften and Bekräftade kopplingar and, from Phase 2, motion links and Återkommande krav that are not certain automatically; today the site owner, working through chat. Phase 1 has no Redaktör work.
_Avoid_: Admin, Moderator

**Veckobatch**:
The weekly numbered set of Föreslagna kopplingar (and, from Phase 2, suggested motion links and Återkommande krav) put to the Redaktör for yes or no.
_Avoid_: Review queue
