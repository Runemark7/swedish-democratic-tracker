## Top candidates
1. **`dittsverige.se`** — emotional, broad, works across all three levels
2. **`dinstat.se`** — sharp, two syllables, best if riksdag stays primary entry point
3. **`dininsyn.se`** — names the legal right citizens have; most principled framing

## Notes
- "Kollen" suffix not required
- Core purpose: deliver government documents (riksdag, myndigheter, region, kommun) to citizens — neutral, sourced, fact-only — because reading what is done in your name is a civic requirement
- Name should signal ownership and accountability, not news aggregation
- Avoid anything that could read as nationalist in isolation
